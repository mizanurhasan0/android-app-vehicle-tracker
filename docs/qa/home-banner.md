## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide mobile transport app dashboard banner background, landscape 3:1 composition. Create a refined minimal teal illustration for PathSathi school vehicle tracking. Deep teal backdrop (#087F78 and #086B67), with a very subtle winding road and small school minibus silhouette toward the far right and lower edge; low-contrast layered hills and tiny route markers. Keep the left two-thirds and upper region almost uniform deep teal, uncluttered negative space for white profile and balance text overlaid by the app. Soft flat illustration, understated, clean, friendly. All shapes monochrome muted teal, no bright highlights. This is background artwork only, no UI, no letters, no numbers, no branding, no watermark, no phone frame. Wide composition with safe center cropping for a compact banner.
